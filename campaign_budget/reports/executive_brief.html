Decision

The selected model does not justify deployment: at a 20% contact budget its test precision is 36.3%, versus 56.2% for a simple prior-success rule. Treat this as a model-risk finding, not a predictive success story. Validate the rule prospectively and pre-register a randomized, consent-compliant pilot with incremental response, opt-outs and subgroup monitoring.

Design

Used 41,188 UCI records in original date order, split 60/20/20. Excluded duration (post-call leakage), current-campaign contact count and demographic/credit attributes. Chose random_forest using validation average precision; retained final 20% for one test.

Budget trade-off

At 20% budget, observed precision is 36.3% versus 30.8% random-selection expectation. A prior-success-history baseline gives 56.2%; both baselines must be reviewed before model complexity is justified.

Economics and sensitivity

Scenario grid assumes 20/50/100 currency units of contribution per observed positive and 1/3/5 per contact. These values are hypothetical, not measured customer lifetime value or marketing ROI; selection bias prevents causal interpretation.

Risks

Historical selected population, temporal/macroeconomic drift, untracked repeated clients, context availability and potential proxy discrimination. A strong AUC alone is insufficient for production.
