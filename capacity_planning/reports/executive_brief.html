Compared calendar-based capacity policies on 2,168 held-out hours. At an assumed 5:1 shortage-to-idle cost, model scenario cost differs by 29.4% from the hour/workday policy (positive means lower cost).

Decision

Test calendar-based capacity guidance in shadow mode before operational use. Review both under-capacity and idle-capacity exposure across the 2:1, 5:1 and 10:1 assumed cost ratios; do not select a policy from mean forecast error alone.

Method

Trained before July 2012, selected tree complexity on July–September 2012 and tested once on October–December 2012. Quantile targets are shortage cost divided by shortage plus idle cost. Compared against constant and hour/workday quantile baselines.

Availability and leakage

Uses calendar and elapsed-time inputs only. Excludes actual weather because future weather is not known without forecasts; excludes registered/casual rentals because they sum to the target. Missing observation hours are not treated as zero demand.

Interpretation

This is an aggregate hourly service-throughput scenario, not an optimal bike fleet or station allocation. Observed completed rentals omit lost demand and do not provide trip duration, fleet inventory or station constraints.

Pilot and safeguards

Validate future availability, operational cost estimates and local demand. Run a shadow pilot with pre-agreed coverage, idle-capacity and stability thresholds; retain a simple policy fallback and review drift monthly.
